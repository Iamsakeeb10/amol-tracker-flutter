# Amol Tracker — App Features

**Version:** 1.0.0+1

Welcome to Amol Tracker. This file highlights the app features in a clean, user-friendly way so you can share it with anyone using the APK.

---

## 🌟 What Amol Tracker Does

Amol Tracker makes it easy to build good Islamic habits every day, stay consistent, and feel motivated through community accountability.

- Track daily Islamic actions with simple on/off buttons.
- See daily results using the Hijri calendar.
- Join a shared community where everyone’s progress is visible.
- Keep going with streaks, badges, reminders, and dua support.

---

## ✅ Daily Habit Tracking

A clear daily checklist helps users stay focused and consistent.

- Log 9 important daily acts of worship.
- Tap each task to mark it complete.
- Use "Mark all done" to finish the day quickly.
- Submit today’s log and keep the day locked.

### Included daily tasks

- Fard prayers (all 5 in congregation)
- Takbir with Takbir-e-Ula
- Morning Azkar
- Evening Azkar
- Quran Tilawat
- Surah Mulk recitation
- Miswak
- Sunnah + Witr prayers
- Post-prayer Azkar

---

## 📊 Progress at a Glance

Users see their performance instantly.

- Points are calculated for each habit.
- Scores are shown in friendly colors:
  - 🟢 Great day
  - 🟡 Good/average day
  - 🔴 Needs improvement
- A completion summary appears after logging.

---

## 🔥 Streaks & Rewards

Keep motivation strong with streaks and rewards.

- Track consecutive days of activity.
- See your best streak.
- Use one weekly streak freeze to protect progress.
- Earn milestone badges for longer streaks.

---

## 📅 History & Hijri Calendar

Review your journey with a full history screen.

- View past days using a Hijri calendar.
- See color-coded results for every day.
- Tap any past date to review details.

---

## 🌍 Community Sheet

A public community dashboard keeps everyone motivated.

- View daily progress from all users.
- See each person’s checked tasks and total score.
- Current user is highlighted for easy tracking.
- Real-time updates show today’s activity instantly.
- Search by display name.
- Anonymous users remain visible with protected identity.

---

## 📰 Activity Feed

Stay inspired with community updates.

- View live messages about completed days.
- Celebrate streak milestones.
- See daily encouragement and Islamic quotes.
- Receive dua notifications from other users.
- Daily hadith of the day is shown to keep motivation spiritually grounded.

---

## 🤝 Public Profiles & Dua

Encourage others and connect through dua.

- Open any user’s public profile.
- View name, avatar, streaks, and stats.
- Send a dua with one tap.
- Dua delivery is limited to once per recipient per day.
- Received duas appear as notifications for easy access.

---

## 🏆 Leaderboard

Friendly social motivation with a public leaderboard.

- See top users in the community.
- Track your rank and performance.
- Public ranking keeps motivation visible.

---

## 🔔 Notifications & Reminders

Never miss a day.

- In-app notification center for alerts, messages, and dua notices.
- Unread notification badge keeps important items visible.
- Mark notifications as read with one tap.
- Morning reminders to start the day with intention.
- Evening reminders to complete today’s log and reflect.
- Additional reminder times can be configured in settings.
- Quiet hours / do not disturb prevents reminders during rest.
- Notification permission is requested during onboarding.
- Firebase push notifications via FCM deliver dua and app alerts.
- Local reminders work even when the app is closed.

---

## ⚙️ Settings & Privacy

Users can personalize their experience.

- Set quiet hours / do not disturb.
- Manage reminder times including morning and evening reminders.
- Choose anonymous community display.
- Access profile and app settings from the More hub.
